### Why have a site like this now that we can learn with AI?

This is a fair question that is worth addressing, and is a bit of the elephant in the room. With AI, we have access to any specific piece of information in a matter of seconds. What's the point of learning from this site as a human reader?

Firstly, you absolutely can point your AI at this site! It has an llm.txt and agents can learn from it.

Secondly, and mainly, I think AI has actually made this kind of learning content more important, not less. AI is prone to [popularity bias](https://hbr.org/2026/03/researchers-asked-llms-for-strategic-advice-they-got-trendslop-in-return), and may not always provide a cohesive testing strategy in the big picture, [especially if you don't know what kind of questions to ask to start with.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/There_are_unknown_unknowns). So, opinionated advice based on real experience that suggests specific libraries and patterns to address specific problems is *more* valuable now, not less.

I also personally enjoy the concept of testing, writing about it, talking about it, and learning about it, so making this content was itself an enjoyable experience for me, anyway.
